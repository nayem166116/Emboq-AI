THE PERCEPTION STACK

Three layers, one continuous read of the physical world.

01

Perceive

Fuse vision, vibration, thermal, and PLC signal data into a single spatial model of each machine and cell — refreshed continuously, not sampled in batches.

02

Predict

Every asset gets a rolling forecast of wear, drift, and failure risk, trained on your own equipment rather than a generic fleet average.

03

Act

Recommendations route straight into your MES, CMMS, or PLC logic — or trigger a supervised stop when a safety envelope is at risk.

THE STACK, RUNNING

One perception layer, every machine on the line.

Perceive, predict, and act run as a single continuous loop at the edge — not three tools stitched together after the fact.

Perception, prediction, and decision layers running together on a live cell.

A WORKING PRINCIPLE

WHERE IT RUNS

THE HORIZON LINE

Perception has always had a horizon. We're moving it deeper into the physical world.

Every Opi Robotics deployment extends that line a little further — from a single cell, to a line, to a site, to a fleet.

DEPLOYMENT

Live on your floor in weeks, not quarters.

01

Instrument

We map your existing sensors and PLC tags first — most sites need little to no new hardware to start.

02

Calibrate

Models are trained against your equipment's own baseline for 2–4 weeks before any recommendation goes live.

03

Operate

Perception runs continuously at the edge, with a supervised rollout of automated actions once trust thresholds are met.

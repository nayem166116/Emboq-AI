CORE CAPABILITIES

01

Perception fusion

Vision, vibration, thermal, acoustic, and existing PLC/OT tags are fused into a single, continuously updated spatial model of each cell — no separate dashboards per sensor type.

02

Predictive wear modeling

Component-level degradation curves trained against your own equipment's operating history, not a generic OEM duty cycle.

03

Safety envelope enforcement

Defines physical and operational limits per cell; when perception detects a boundary risk, TTA Robotics can trigger a supervised hold before a person or part is at risk.

04

Fleet-wide learning

Patterns learned on one line — a bearing signature, a fastening drift — propagate to comparable equipment across your other sites, with your review before rollout.

05

Integration layer

Native connectors for OPC-UA, Modbus TCP, PROFINET, and MQTT, plus a REST API for MES and CMMS systems you already run.

06

Audit & compliance trail

Every recommendation and automated action is logged with the perception evidence behind it — built for plants that answer to quality and safety audits.

ABOUT

We think machines should understand the space they work in.

Emboq AI was built by engineers who spent years watching perfectly good automation fail for reasons no dashboard ever flagged.

WHY WE EXIST

Most industrial automation can act. Very little of it can actually perceive.

A robot arm can repeat a motion with sub-millimeter precision for a decade. What it usually can't do is notice that the part it's holding has shifted, that a neighboring machine is vibrating differently than it did last month, or that a maintenance window closed three weeks too late. Emboq AI exists to close that gap — giving physical systems a continuous, shared read of the space and equipment around them.

HOW WE WORK

01

We deploy on real floors, not just in pilots

Every model we ship has been validated against production equipment before it reaches a customer — not just a benchmark dataset.

02

We treat safety engineering as a co-owner, not a reviewer

Any capability that can influence a physical action is scoped with a customer's safety team from the first conversation, not after a pilot is already running.

03

We publish our reasoning, including the uncomfortable parts

Our blog includes the failure modes and tradeoffs we've hit, not just the wins — because that's what a buyer actually needs to evaluate us.
